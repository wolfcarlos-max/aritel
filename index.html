Our Story

Made with culture. Worn with pride.

Born from a desire to bring Filipino identity into everyday streetwear, ARITEL was founded. ARITEL is a Filipino streetwear brand drawn from the inspiration of the Philippines' rich culture, history and heritage. Every design tells a story, bringing the beauty of our heritage into pieces made for the present. We make more than just clothes: we emphasize quality, comfort and timeless style. Every piece becomes your canvas for self-expression, where heritage and individuality collide.
