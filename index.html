Our Story

Made with culture. Worn with pride.

Born from a desire to bring Filipino identity into everyday streetwear, ARITEL was founded. ARITEL is a Filipino streetwear brand drawn from the inspiration from the Philippines’ rich culture, history, and heritage. Every design tells a story, bringing the beauty of our roots into wearable pieces made for the present. We make more than just clothes, but to emphasize quality, comfort, and timeless style. Every piece we have becomes uniquely your canvas for self-expression where heritage and individuality collide.
